## App name

LunarLog

## Short description

Private, offline period and wellness tracking with clear cycle estimates.

## Full description

LunarLog is a privacy-first period and wellness tracker that works without an account or subscription.

Track period dates, flow, symptoms, mood, sleep, hydration, temperature, cervical mucus, notes, and medication doses. Review your history in a calendar, explore cycle trends, create a report for your own records, and export or restore a local backup whenever you choose.

Your core tracking data stays on your device. LunarLog has no ads, analytics SDK, or required cloud account. Optional app lock, private reminders, and secure-screen protection help reduce unwanted exposure.

Cycle and fertile-day predictions are estimates based on the data you record. Fertile-day estimates are not birth control.

LunarLog is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
