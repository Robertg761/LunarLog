LunarLog

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

LunarLog is a menstrual cycle and wellness tracking app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data LunarLog handles, how that data is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • LunarLog is designed to work locally on your device.
  • LunarLog does not require you to create an account.
  • LunarLog does not sell your personal data.
  • LunarLog does not use advertising SDKs.
  • LunarLog does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your cycle and wellness entries are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information LunarLog Handles

Depending on how you use the app, LunarLog may handle the following information:

  • Menstrual cycle data, including period start and end dates
  • Wellness and health-related entries, such as symptoms, mood, flow, sleep, hydration, medications, temperature, cervical mucus, and notes
  • App settings, such as reminders, theme preferences, and app lock preferences
  • Locally generated backup files and reports that you choose to export

How Your Information Is Used

LunarLog uses your information to:

  • Store and display your logs on your device
  • Generate insights, predictions, calendars, and history views
  • Create local backups and reports when you request them
  • Send reminder and cycle-related notifications if you enable them
  • Protect access to the app if you enable app lock

Where Data Is Stored

LunarLog stores app data locally on your Android device using on-device storage. The app is configured with Android automatic cloud backup disabled.

LunarLog does not upload your cycle or wellness data to the developer's servers.

Sharing and Exporting

LunarLog only shares your information when you choose to do so.

Examples include:

  • Exporting a backup file in JSON format
  • Saving a generated PDF or CSV report
  • Using Android share features you trigger manually
  • Saving files to a location or service you choose, such as local storage or a connected cloud storage provider available through Android

Once you export or share data outside the app, that copy is governed by the privacy and security practices of the destination you choose.

Permissions and Device Features

LunarLog may use the following Android permissions or device features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: used only to send reminder or cycle notifications that you enable
  • Biometric or device credentials: used only for app lock and handled through Android's authentication system
  • Local file access through Android's document picker and file APIs: used only when you export, restore, or install an update you requested
  • Home screen widget support: if you add the widget, limited cycle-related information may be visible on your device's home screen

LunarLog does not receive or store your raw fingerprint, face scan, or other biometric template data. Biometric authentication is processed by Android and only the success or failure result is returned to the app.

Network Use

LunarLog is designed for offline use. Core tracking features do not require an internet connection.

For Google Play builds, LunarLog's core health tracking features work without sending your health data to the developer.

For direct-download GitHub builds, LunarLog may contact GitHub to check for app updates and download a newer APK if you choose to install one. That update flow does not upload your cycle or wellness entries.

Third Parties

LunarLog does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics services.

Depending on your choices, limited data may be processed by third parties you interact with directly through your device, such as:

  • Google Play, when you download or update the Play Store version
  • GitHub, if you use a direct-download version that checks for updates
  • Your chosen file storage or sharing destination when you export or share files
  • Android system services used for notifications, biometric authentication, and file selection

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or use the in-app data deletion features.

LunarLog includes options to:

  • Export your data
  • Restore data from a backup you choose
  • Delete all app data using the in-app reset feature

Children's Privacy

LunarLog is not designed to knowingly collect personal information online from children because the app does not require accounts and stores data locally on-device. If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information outside the intended local use of the app, please contact the developer using the contact method below.

If you choose to make LunarLog available to children under 13, you are responsible for ensuring your store listing, disclosures, and app configuration comply with applicable laws and platform requirements.

Security

LunarLog is built to reduce unnecessary data exposure by keeping health data on-device and by offering optional app lock protection. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for protecting access to your device and exported files.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When the policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the new policy URL or repository location with a revised effective date.
